## 📊 Amazon E-Commerce Analysis using Power BI

An end-to-end **Power BI analytics project** built on Amazon sales data, designed to uncover key revenue drivers, customer behavior patterns, and operational inefficiencies across categories and products through a fully interactive, 3-tab report.

---

## 📌 Overview

Developed a 3-tab Power BI report to analyze Amazon sales data and identify key revenue drivers, customer behavior patterns, and operational inefficiencies across categories and products.

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis

- Designed a 3-layer dashboard (Business Overview, Product Category Analysis, Individual Product Analysis)
- Performed Year-over-Year (YoY) growth analysis using DAX
- Analyzed customer demand, delivery efficiency, and pricing vs revenue relationships
- Evaluated customer dissatisfaction drivers and product-level performance

---

## 🗂️ Report Structure

### 1️⃣ Business Performance Overview *(Main Tab)*
- Revenue, Orders, Customers, Customer Satisfaction
- Revenue distribution across locations
- Category trends and monthly performance
- Customer dissatisfaction drivers

### 2️⃣ Product Category Performance Analysis
- Customer demand by category
- Delivery efficiency comparison
- YoY growth analysis
- Pricing vs revenue relationship

### 3️⃣ Individual Product Performance Analysis
- Product-level sales and YoY comparison
- Top-performing products
- Customer satisfaction vs revenue segmentation

---

## 📈 Insights

- 💄 **Health & Beauty** and **Fashion** contribute over **55%** of total demand
- ⚡ **Electronics** shows the highest growth (**47%**) but lower revenue efficiency
- ⚠️ Customer dissatisfaction is driven by product quality issues (**5.8K – 6.1K cases**)
- 🚚 Delivery performance is consistent (**9.5 days**) across categories
- 💰 Revenue is concentrated among a limited number of high-performing products
